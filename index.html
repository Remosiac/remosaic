Remosaic

Flash colors, match sets, unlock the picture. Classic path, daily seed, endless climb — or your own photo.

How to play

  1. Click pieces to flash their color (and pattern, if enabled).
  2. Match a full set of the same color — pairs, triples, or quads.
  3. 2 in a row — hit the same color twice in a row. If you switch colors before that, your last fully solved color comes back onto the board (one step back, not a full reset).
  4. Matched pieces clear and reveal the picture underneath.
  5. Clear the board to unlock the image and finish the level.

Modes

  • Classic / Kids / Challenge — same journey, different difficulty knobs.
  • Daily — one seeded puzzle per calendar day (compare scores with friends).
  • Endless — infinite waves; board and match size ramp up.
  • Your photo — hide a personal image under the mosaic.

Twists & helpers

  • Preview / Ghost / Focus / Peeks change per level.
  • Patterns (settings) add stripes & dots for colorblind-friendly play.
  • P peek · R restart
